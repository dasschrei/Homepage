Our bottle list counts over 500 wines and is updated continuously from our cellar. The focus is on Austria and France, alongside Germany and Italy.

France: Burgundy, Champagne with many grower Champagnes, Loire, Rhône and Jura. Austria: Wachau, Southern Styria, Leithaberg, Thermenregion, Neusiedlersee and Kamptal. Germany: Rheinhessen and Pfalz. Italy: Piedmont and Tuscany.

Sparkling, white, rosé, red and sweet wines, with selected bottles in magnum. Prices per bottle range from about 30 to 600 euros.
