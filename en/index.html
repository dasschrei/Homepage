The experience

Less talk. More taste.

You choose how many courses you’d like. We take care of the rest.

Our surprise menu is shaped by the season, the produce and our craft. It changes constantly – not because change is fashionable, but because it comes naturally.

Three spaces

One evening,
three stages.

  • The dining room 01
  • The chef’s table 02
  • The garden 03
01 — The dining room

Calm. Warm.
Relaxed.

Clean modern lines, light maple tables, steel details and exposed brick set a relaxed stage for the evening. In the dining room, you decide how many courses it should be.

02 — The chef’s table

Right at the heart of it.

Seated directly at the open kitchen, you see every move and watch the full tasting menu come together. The evening starts together for all chef’s table guests at 7 pm.

Starts 7 pm · 7 courses
03 — The garden

Out in the green

In the warm summer months, we open our garden. Beneath old chestnut and willow trees, you can enjoy your aperitif here or the whole menu outdoors – right in Salzburg, yet surprisingly quiet.

Wine & drinks

Origin in the glass. Craft at the centre.

Our pairings are created alongside the menu. Every combination is considered from the very start – so that dish and wine say more together than either could on its own.

Our non-alcoholic pairings are developed entirely in-house. Using ferments, teas, juices and seasonal ingredients, we create drinks with a character of their own – made with the same care, curiosity and dedication as our wine selection.
